# 11. Money: quotes, documents, invoices, payments, products, checkout, accounting sync, reporting

Research date: 2026-11-06. Scope: money features in small-business all-in-one platforms, with Stripe as the processor reference and PandaDoc and Docusign as e-signature references.

How to read this file:

- Every row cites a source URL. OBSERVED means the claim is stated on the cited page. INFERRED means a reasonable reading across sources, or a synthesis for our own design, and is labeled as such.
- "Not confirmed" means we could not reach an official page that states it. Several vendor help centers (TaxDome help, Jobber app marketplace, Fresha membership and gift card help, HubSpot subscriptions and QuickBooks sync pages, Capterra and G2) returned 403 or 404 during this pass. Those gaps are listed explicitly rather than filled from memory.
- Pricing numbers are US list prices as published on the day of research and change often.
- Method note: the shared web search budget for this session was exhausted after 18 searches; the rest of the evidence (roughly 120 page fetches) came from direct fetches of official help centers, docs and pricing pages.

Product short names used in tables: HL = HighLevel, JB = Jobber, HCP = Housecall Pro, TD = TaxDome, FR = Fresha, MM = Mangomint, SQ = Square, KP = Keap, TH = Thryv (ThryvPay), HS = HubSpot Commerce Hub, ST = Stripe, PD = PandaDoc, DS = Docusign.

## Part 9. Synthesized data model

INFERRED. This model is a synthesis of Stripe's objects with the behaviors the all-in-ones add (deposits, optional lines, documents and signatures, tips, prepaid balances). Money amounts are integers in minor units with a currency code. Every object carries org_id (tenant), created_at, updated_at and metadata.

### Product
- id, org_id, name, description, type (service | good | fee | gift_card | package | membership), tax_code, default_price_id, active, images[], sku, unit_label (hour, visit, each), track_inventory (bool), metadata.
- Rule: a different row on a price list is a different Product; a different interval or currency is a different Price. Archive, never delete.

### Price
- id, product_id, currency, unit_amount, billing_scheme (per_unit | tiered), tiers[], type (one_time | recurring), recurring {interval: day | week | month | year, interval_count, usage_type: licensed | metered}, tax_behavior (inclusive | exclusive | unspecified), lookup_key, active, nickname.
- Immutable once used. Price changes create a new Price and archive the old.

### Customer
- id, org_id, name, email, phone, billing_address, shipping_address, tax_exempt (none | exempt | reverse), tax_ids[], preferred_locale, invoice_prefix, next_invoice_sequence, default_payment_method_id, credit_balance (minor units, can be negative to grant credit), processor_customer_ids {stripe: cus_..}, portal_access.

### Quote
- id, org_id, customer_id, number (prefix-seq-revision), revision, parent_quote_id (for revisions), status (draft | open | accepted | declined | changes_requested | expired | canceled | converted), template_id, header, footer, memo, terms_text, expires_at, currency, collection_method (charge_automatically | send_invoice), days_until_due, default_tax_rate_ids[], discounts[] (coupon_id or ad hoc amount/percent), deposit {type: percent | fixed, value, due: on_acceptance | date}, payment_schedule[] {percent_or_amount, due_at}, financing_enabled, totals {subtotal, discount, tax, total, upfront_total, recurring_total_per_interval}, sent_at, viewed_at, accepted_at, accepted_by {name, email, ip, user_agent}, signature_id, converted_to {invoice_id | subscription_id | job_id}, salesperson_user_id, attachments[], client_change_requests[] {text, created_at}.
- Status rules: only expires_at editable while open; editing a signed quote clears signature_id, bumps revision and stores the prior PDF.

### QuoteLine
- id, quote_id, position, product_id, price_id (nullable for ad hoc), description, quantity, unit_amount, unit_label, is_optional, is_selected (client choice, default true when pre-selected), tier_group (for good/better/best: group key plus is_selected per group), discounts[], tax_rate_ids[], show_unit_price, show_quantity, image_url, line_total, internal_cost.

### Document
- id, org_id, customer_id, quote_id (nullable), invoice_id (nullable), type (contract | proposal | engagement_letter | agreement | waiver | other), template_id, title, content (blocks or PDF file), merge_values {}, fields[] {id, type: signature | initials | text | date | checkbox, recipient_id, page, x, y, required}, recipients[] {id, name, email, role: signer | approver | cc | staff_countersigner, order}, signing_order (sequential | parallel), status (draft | sent | viewed | partially_signed | completed | declined | expired | voided), expires_at, payment {collect_on_sign, product_lines[], one_time_or_recurring}, completed_pdf_url, certificate_pdf_url, reminder_schedule.

### Signature
- id, document_id, recipient_id, signed_at, ip, user_agent, method (drawn | typed | uploaded | in_person), auth_level (email_link | sms_otp | access_code | kba | id_verification), consent_text_version, consent_accepted_at, audit_events[] {type: sent | viewed | signed | declined | reminded, at, ip}, hash_of_signed_content.
- Keep the ESIGN consumer consent record (disclosures shown, consent captured electronically) with the signature.

### Invoice
- id, org_id, customer_id, number, status (draft | open | partially_paid (derived) | paid | void | uncollectible), collection_method, due_at, days_until_due, currency, subtotal, discount_total, tax_total, total, amount_paid, amount_remaining, amount_overpaid, deposit_applied, memo, footer, custom_fields[] (max 4), default_tax_rate_ids[], discounts[], payment_schedule[] {amount, due_at, status}, allow_partial_payment, auto_advance, next_payment_attempt_at, attempt_count, late_fee_policy_id, reminder_schedule_id, source {quote_id | subscription_id | job_id | document_id | appointment_id}, period_start, period_end, hosted_url, hosted_url_expires_at, pdf_url, tip_amount, surcharge_amount (with disclosure_text and method restriction), finalized_at, paid_at, voided_at, marked_uncollectible_at, payments[] (InvoicePayment: payment_id, amount_applied, status), credit_notes[].
- Terminal states: paid (reopenable by unapplying), void. Credit notes reduce amount_remaining; a zero balance moves open to paid.

### InvoiceLine
- id, invoice_id, position, product_id, price_id, description, quantity, unit_amount, discounts[], tax_rate_ids[], tax_amounts[] {tax_rate_id, amount, inclusive}, amount, period {start, end}, proration (bool), group_label, source_quote_line_id, staff_user_id (for revenue by staff and commissions).

### CreditNote
- id, invoice_id, number, reason (duplicate | fraudulent | order_change | product_unsatisfactory | other), lines[] {invoice_line_id | custom, quantity or amount, tax_rate_ids}, total, refund_amount, credit_balance_amount, out_of_band_amount, status (issued | void), pdf_url.

### Payment
- id, org_id, customer_id, invoice_id (nullable; a Payment can apply to several invoices via InvoicePayment), amount, currency, status (requires_action | processing | succeeded | failed | canceled), method_type (card | ach_debit | sepa | wallet | cash | check | bank_transfer | gift_card | account_balance | financing | external), payment_method_id, processor (stripe | square | lc | keap_pay | manual), processor_payment_id, processor_fee, net_amount, captured_at, settled_at, payout_id, tip_amount, surcharge_amount, channel (hosted_page | payment_link | text_to_pay | pos_reader | tap_to_pay | card_on_file | manual), mandate_id (bank debit), receipt_url, refunds[], dispute_id, external_reference (check number).

### PaymentMethod
- id, customer_id, type (card | us_bank_account | sepa_debit | link | wallet), brand, last4, exp_month, exp_year, funding (credit | debit | prepaid), fingerprint, billing_details, allow_redisplay (always | limited), mandate {id, type: one_time | recurring, accepted_at, ip, user_agent, text_version, status: active | inactive}, processor_pm_id, is_default_for_invoices, network_token_or_updater_flag.
- Never store PAN or CVC. Store only what the processor returns.

### Refund
- id, payment_id, amount, currency, reason, status (pending | requires_action | succeeded | failed | canceled), failure_reason, processor_refund_id, reference (ARN), credit_note_id, created_by_user_id, created_at, processor_fee_returned (false for Stripe).

### Dispute
- id, payment_id, amount, fee, reason_code, status (needs_response | under_review | won | lost | warning_closed), evidence_due_by, evidence[], network (visa | mastercard | amex | ach_return), funds_withdrawn_at, funds_reinstated_at.

### Subscription (also Membership)
- id, org_id, customer_id, status (incomplete | incomplete_expired | trialing | active | past_due | unpaid | paused | canceled), items[] {price_id, quantity, current_trial, included_credits}, collection_method, default_payment_method_id, billing_cycle_anchor, current_period_start, current_period_end, trial_end, pause_collection {behavior: keep_as_draft | mark_uncollectible | void, resumes_at}, cancel_at, cancel_at_period_end, canceled_at, cancellation_details {reason, feedback}, proration_behavior_default, discounts[], agreement_document_id, latest_invoice_id, retry_policy_id, end_behavior_after_retries (cancel | unpaid | leave_past_due), entitlements[] (services per period, dollar balance per period, discount percent), credit_ledger[] {period, granted, used, expires_at, rollover}.

### Coupon and PromotionCode
- Coupon: id, name, percent_off | amount_off (per currency), duration (once | repeating | forever), duration_in_months, max_redemptions, redeem_by, applies_to_product_ids[], times_redeemed, valid.
- PromotionCode: id, coupon_id, code (case-insensitive unique while active), customer_id (optional), first_time_transaction_only, minimum_amount, expires_at, max_redemptions, active.
- Discount (applied instance): id, coupon_id or promotion_code_id, target (customer | subscription | invoice | line), start, end.

### TaxRate
- id, org_id, display_name, percentage (4 decimals), inclusive, jurisdiction, country, state, description, active, effective_from, tax_type (sales_tax | vat | gst | hst | other). Immutable percentage; archive and recreate on change.

### Payout
- id, org_id, processor, processor_payout_id, amount, currency, status (pending | in_transit | paid | failed | canceled), method (standard | instant), arrival_date, fee (instant payouts), bank_account_last4, trace_id, balance_transactions[] {type: charge | refund | dispute | fee | adjustment | transfer, gross, fee, net, source_id, invoice_id, payment_id}, reconciled_at, accounting_journal_id.

### Supporting objects
- GiftCard: id, code, initial_value, balance, owner_customer_id, purchaser_customer_id, expires_at (null where law forbids expiry), status, promotion_id, transactions[].
- Package: product_id, included_services[] {service_id, quantity}, expires_days, redemptions[].
- Deposit: modeled as a Payment with purpose = deposit applied to an open Invoice (or to a quote until the invoice exists). Report as a liability until the sale closes.
- Tip: an amount on Payment and Invoice attributed to staff_user_id, excluded from revenue and from tax.
- Surcharge or convenience fee: a separate line with type fee, disclosure text, method restriction (credit only), rate cap check per region.

### Key relations
Customer 1..n Quote, Invoice, Subscription, PaymentMethod, Document. Quote 1..n QuoteLine; Quote 0..1 Document (proposal) ; Quote converts to 0..1 Invoice or Subscription. Invoice 1..n InvoiceLine, 0..n InvoicePayment (to Payment), 0..n CreditNote. Payment 0..n Refund, 0..1 Dispute, 0..1 Payout. Subscription 1..n Invoice. Document 1..n Signature.

## Part 11. What this tells us a template must have

Priority legend: TABLE STAKES (every reference product has it and users expect it on day one), EXPECTED (most references have it; missing it will be noticed within the first month), DIFFERENTIATOR (few have it well; it wins deals). Each item lists the prerequisite.

### Table stakes
1. Product catalog with separate Price objects, one-time and recurring, tax behavior per price, archive-not-delete. Prerequisite: none. (Stripe products and prices.)
2. Quotes with line items from the catalog plus ad hoc lines, discounts (percent and amount), tax rates, expiration date, PDF, send by email and SMS, client accept or decline with a note, statuses draft, sent, accepted, declined, expired, converted. Prerequisite: email and SMS sending. (Stripe, Jobber, HighLevel.)
3. One-click conversion from accepted quote to invoice, and to subscription when any line is recurring, with the quote frozen as a snapshot and revision numbers. (Stripe, Jobber.)
4. Invoices with Stripe's state machine (draft, open, paid, void, uncollectible), numbering with prefix and start number, due terms, memo, footer, custom fields, PDF, hosted pay page, email with PDF attached, automatic reminders before, on and after due. (Stripe, HubSpot, HighLevel ideas board.)
5. Payments: cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, ACH with mandate text and verification, card on file with consent, auto-charge, manual recording of cash, check and external payments with receipts. Prerequisite: Stripe Connect account per tenant (see recommendation), ACH capability requested. (Stripe ACH, hosted invoice page.)
6. Partial payments and deposits: amount_remaining, InvoicePayment records, partially paid status visible to the client, deposit at quote approval that auto-applies to the final invoice, deposits reported as liabilities until the sale closes. (Stripe partial payments, Jobber, Mangomint.)
7. Refunds (full and partial, to original method only) and credit notes (line-level by amount or quantity, refund vs credit balance vs out of band), void only after refund. (Stripe credit notes and refunds.)
8. Tax rates with inclusive and exclusive handling, per-line overrides, exempt and reverse charge, line vs invoice rounding, exportable tax summary. (Stripe tax rates.)
9. Receipts, hosted page link lifetime rules, and a client portal listing invoices, balances and saved methods. (Stripe hosted invoice page, Jobber client hub.)
10. Processor-grade reporting: sales, outstanding and aging, payments collected, fees, payouts with itemized reconciliation, taxes collected. (Stripe payout reconciliation, Jobber reports.)
11. PCI posture: card entry only through processor-hosted elements or certified readers, store brand, last4, expiry only; annual attestation reminder in settings. (Stripe security guide.)

### Expected
12. Payment schedules and installments (percent or fixed, due on acceptance or dates) and milestone billing against a job. (HighLevel, Square Plus, ThryvPay.)
13. Optional line items and good/better/best groups with live total recalculation, percentage deposits recomputed on selection, unselected items dropped at conversion. (Jobber, Housecall Pro, PandaDoc.)
14. Documents and e-signature with templates and merge fields, signature, initials, text, date and checkbox fields, sequential or parallel signers, staff counter-signature, completion certificate with email, IP, timestamp and audit events, ESIGN consent capture, payment at signing. Prerequisite: durable PDF rendering and hashing. (HighLevel, Docusign, HubSpot, 15 USC 7001.)
15. Subscriptions and memberships: statuses mirroring Stripe, trials with missing-payment-method behavior, proration preview, pause collection with invoice behavior, cancel at period end, Smart Retries with configurable end state, dunning emails, near one-click cancel link for FTC and California compliance. (Stripe Billing, Mangomint.)
16. Text to pay and reusable payment links with QR codes. (HighLevel, Stripe Payment Links, Mangomint self checkout.)
17. Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android and at least one certified reader, with tipping prompts and on-reader input. Prerequisite: Terminal SDK in the mobile app, Apple entitlement, "How to Tap" overlay. (Stripe Terminal, Fresha, Jobber, Housecall Pro.)
18. Coupons and promotion codes with duration, redemption limits, first-time and minimum-amount restrictions, product scoping, stacking order rules. (Stripe coupons.)
19. Gift cards and packages as prepaid liabilities with partial redemption, balance history and liability reports. (Square, Mangomint.)
20. QuickBooks Online and Xero sync using a processor clearing account, mapping customers, items, tax rates and payouts, with fees as expenses and deposits matched to payouts; never sync a deposit invoice and a final invoice at full value. Prerequisite: stable external IDs on every object. (Housecall Pro, HubSpot limits, Stripe reconciliation report.)
21. Dispute workflow: webhook-driven case list, evidence deadline, fee display, ACH dispute handling that disables the mandate. (Stripe disputes and ACH.)
22. Quote follow-up automation and quote conversion reporting (conversion rate, sent vs converted, opens). (Jobber.)

### Differentiators
23. Consumer financing embedded in quotes and invoices (Wisetack-style prequalification without hard pull, monthly "as low as" on tiered proposals), with the merchant fee shown transparently. Prerequisite: lender partnership; US only for Wisetack. (Jobber, Housecall Pro.)
24. Compliant surcharging engine: credit-only, debit excluded, per-region cap and ban table, disclosure text on pay page and receipt, 30-day acquirer notice checklist, separate convenience fee and cash discount modes, per-method fee rules (card vs ACH). (HighLevel pain points, Visa, state statutes.)
25. Deposit and no-show policy engine for appointment businesses: fixed or percent deposits, card capture only, per-booking override, fee windows, irreversible charge warnings, client notifications, deposit shown on the final invoice. (Fresha, Mangomint.)
26. POS layer: split payments across methods, cash drawer with daily closeout, tips split across staff, product returns, voids, self checkout by text. (Mangomint.)
27. Prepaid balance and account credit ledger usable at checkout and on invoices, with expiry and rollover rules. (Mangomint account balance, Stripe credit balance.)
28. KBA or ID verification as an optional signature step for regulated verticals, priced per use. (TaxDome, Docusign Identify.)
29. Revision-aware quotes with signature invalidation and prior-version PDFs retained, plus client change requests as a first-class status. (Jobber, Stripe quote numbering.)
30. Platform fee reporting for the operator (application fees per tenant) and per-tenant payout schedules. (Stripe Connect.)

### Processor recommendation and prerequisites
- INFERRED. Use Stripe Connect with direct charges and on_behalf_of semantics (connected account is merchant of record, own statement descriptor, own payout schedule, own refunds and disputes), charging an application fee if the platform wants revenue share; Stripe explicitly names "an accounting platform that enables invoice payments" as the direct-charge SaaS case. Keep a manual-payment path (cash, check, external) and an "external processor" record type so businesses that keep Square or PayPal for POS can still reconcile.
- Prerequisites to ship payments: Connect platform profile, card_payments and us_bank_account_ach_payments capabilities, ACH mandate text and Nacha mandate emails, hosted invoice page save-payment-method setting at "offer", Terminal entitlement from Apple for Tap to Pay, webhook endpoint with signature verification for invoice, payment, refund, dispute, subscription and payout events, payout reconciliation report ingestion, Radar.
- Compliance prerequisites: ESIGN consumer consent flow and record retention for signed documents; FTC and California one-click cancel for subscriptions; privacy terms for saving payment methods; surcharge legality table by state and network notice; PCI annual attestation; tax invoice rules by jurisdiction (account-level sequential numbering in EU and UK).
