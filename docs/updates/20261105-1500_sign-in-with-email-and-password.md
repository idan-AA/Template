## 2026-11-05: Sign in with email and password

You no longer need Google, Microsoft or SSO to get in.

- **Deploy it and get in.** On a brand-new deploy the sign-in page asks you to create the owner account (name, email, password). That form disappears once the first account exists.
- **Add your people.** Settings, Members, Add member: name, email, role and a temporary password (Generate makes a good one). No email is sent; you pass the password on yourself, and they choose their own the first time they sign in.
- **Reset a password.** In the Members row menu, Reset password sets a new temporary one and signs that person out everywhere.
- **Change your own.** Settings, Account.
- **Setup never blocks you.** The company website is optional, and the research key step only appears when the CRM is on and can always be skipped.
